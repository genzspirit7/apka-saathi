    How to install

    📱 Android
    1. Tap Download for Android and wait for the download to finish.
    2. Open the file. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app.
    3. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details and then Install anyway.
    4. Open Apka Saathi, create your account and verify your email.
    🪟 Windows
    1. Click Download for Windows and wait for the .zip file.
    2. Right-click the zip, choose Extract All, and extract it to a folder (do not run it from inside the zip).
    3. Open the folder and double-click apka_saathi.exe.
    4. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info and then Run anyway.
    5. Create your account or log in. You can right-click the exe and choose Send to > Desktop (create shortcut).

    Questions

    Is Apka Saathi free?

    Yes. The app is free to download and use.

    Why do I see a warning when installing?

    The app is shared as a direct download and is not in an app store yet, so Android (Play Protect) and Windows (SmartScreen) ask for your confirmation. Only install the file from this page.

    The Windows app does not open. What should I do?

    Make sure you extracted the whole zip and run the exe from the extracted folder. If it still does not open, install the free “Microsoft Visual C++ Redistributable (x64)” from Microsoft and try again.

    How do I update the app?

    Android: download the newest APK from this page and install it over the old one. Windows: download the newest zip, extract it and use the new folder (you can delete the old one). Your account and progress are saved online, so you do not lose them.

    Can I use it on an iPhone or Mac?

    Not right now. The app is available for Android phones and Windows PCs.

    Privacy

    What we store: your name, class and email, your study progress (study time, XP, streak, goals), quiz and event answers, and the posts, comments, chats and reports you create in the community.

    Where it is stored: on Google Firebase servers. We do not sell your data.

    Who can see what: your name, XP and streak can be seen by other students on the leaderboard. Personal chats are visible only to the people in that chat. Moderators can review reported content.

    Your choices: you can make your account private, block users and report content inside the app. To ask for your data to be deleted, write to our support email.
